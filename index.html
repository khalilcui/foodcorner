About Us

Food Corner is a friendly neighbourhood restaurant serving freshly prepared pizza, burgers, sandwiches and pasta. We use quality ingredients, cook every order to demand and believe that a good meal should be affordable, warm and served with a smile.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick lunch, a family dinner or a late-evening snack, our team is happy to welcome you. Dine in, take away or call ahead, your table is always ready.
